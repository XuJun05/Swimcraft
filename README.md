## 🌊 Overview
**Swimcraft** is a Fabric mod that adds 23 diverse types of swimsuits and accessories to your Minecraft world! 
More than just cosmetics, each item grants **special status effects when equipped**, making your underwater exploration and survival life more comfortable and unique!

---

## 🩲 Features & Effects
All items are equipped in the legs slot.
They all grant **Water Breathing**, making them perfect for exploring oceans and rivers.

### 🩳 Trunks
* **Colors**: White, Blue, Green, Red, Yellow
* **Effects**: Water Breathing I, Dolphin's Grace II

### 🥊 Boxers
* **Colors**: White, Dark, Lime, Purple
* **Effects**: Water Breathing I, Dolphin's Grace II, **Jump Boost II**

### 🩲 Briefs
* **Colors**: White, Black, Blue
* **Effects**: Water Breathing I, Dolphin's Grace II, **Speed II**

### 🏊 Racing Briefs
* **Colors**: Blue, Black, Life Saver (Red)
* **Effects**: Water Breathing I, **Dolphin's Grace III**
* *The Life Saver Racing Brief also grants **Regeneration I**!*

### 🎌 Fundoshi
Traditional Japanese accessories and swimwear! Features both "Etchu" and "Rokushaku" styles.
* **Colors**: White, Red
* **Effects**: Water Breathing I, **Dolphin's Grace III**, **Strength II**

---

## 🛠️ Crafting
All swimsuits and accessories are fully craftable in survival mode!
Combine **Wool** (matching the item's color) with **String** or **Leather**. Check your recipe book for the exact patterns.
